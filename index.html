Evidence should be usable.

HECAVEX Labs turns selected research into small, inspectable tools. Each output keeps its source and evidence boundary visible. No black-box score, no magic attribution button.

Three useful, deliberately narrow tools

These are research aids, not automated verdicts. Their limitations are part of the interface.

Lab 01 · sourced observations

Baltic Threat Atlas

Filter selected public cyber observations concerning Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia. Each record links to the national authority or joint advisory behind it.

Lab 03 · regulatory triage

CRA reporting assistant

A conservative decision aid for Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 reporting preparation. It points to questions and deadlines, never a legal conclusion.

Run the triage →
Evidence boundary. The atlas is a selected public-source dataset, the graph is one published case, and the CRA assistant is educational. None is an exhaustive threat feed, blocklist, incident-response service or substitute for legal advice.
