OSINT Workbench resources

The generic tool directory is no longer a primary Labs workspace. Its reviewed snapshot remains available so older citations and research notes do not disappear.

ARCHIVED 26 AUG 2026 · LAST CURATED 14 AUG 2026

Why this changed. A small static list duplicated larger, actively maintained community catalogues and did not add enough HECAVEX-specific analytical value. Labs now focuses on the Baltic Threat Atlas, Evidence Pivot Workspace and ATT&CK Evidence Explorer.

Preserved resource snapshot

The dated list of 39 free and free-tier resources is retained as archived JSON. Provider availability, access models, terms and limits may have changed since the last review. Inclusion was never endorsement, and the snapshot receives no routine maintenance.

Where to look now

For a maintained general-purpose directory, use the community-supported Bellingcat Online Investigation Toolkit ↗. For HECAVEX-specific method and evidence cautions, use the Labs methodology and the workflow notes embedded in each maintained workspace.

Compatibility and corrections

This URL remains stable for historical links. The archived dataset remains represented in the data catalogue and product retirement is recorded in Changes. Corrections to the preserved record can be sent through the HECAVEX contact channel.
