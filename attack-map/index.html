What the labels mean

REPORTED

A source states the behaviour

The relationship remains attributed to the named publisher. HECAVEX has reviewed and structured it, not independently observed the intrusion.

OBSERVED

HECAVEX holds direct evidence

Reserved for evidence collected or reproduced during a documented HECAVEX investigation. None of the initial actor mappings use this label.

ASSESSED

An analytical inference is explicit

Used only when the relationship is reasoned from stated evidence and limitations. It must never be visually confused with source reporting.
