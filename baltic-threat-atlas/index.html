Before comparing countries

Three counts, three different measurements

Question: do these figures establish which country faced more cyber risk? No. The reporting periods, inclusion rules and visibility differ, and comparable population denominators are not established.

Lithuania · 2025 · 2,888 records

The published total includes 19 major incidents, 380 minor incidents and 2,489 near misses. It is an annual reporting total, not a compromise rate. Inspect definition and source.

Latvia · Q1 2026 · 846 manually processed incidents

This quarterly unit differs from the separate device-visibility count. CERT.LV describes expanded SOC visibility and reporting coverage. Inspect definition and source.

Estonia · June 2026 · 1,232 incidents with impact

This is one month with an impact-based inclusion rule, not the same measurement as either preceding count. Inspect definition and source.

Decision: exclude from country ranking. Do not divide these counts by each other or annualise them into a prevalence claim. First establish equivalent units, populations, period coverage, reporting definitions and visibility changes. Every downloadable observation now carries this comparison boundary and an explicit date basis, including unknowns.

Method

How to read the dataset

reported

One record, one claim

Each row captures a bounded public statement. Separate incidents are not merged into a campaign merely because they occurred nearby.

source attributed

Attribution stays attributed

Government or vendor language remains attached to its source. HECAVEX does not silently upgrade it into independent certainty.

coverage limit

Absence is not prevalence

The dataset favours public authorities and selected material. Missing records describe coverage, not threat absence.
