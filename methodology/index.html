Evidence and publication standard

Methodology and limitations

Labs makes selected HECAVEX research inspectable without turning reference data, technical similarity or automation into unsupported conclusions.

MAINTAINED · REVIEWED 26 AUG 2026

Purpose and audience

HECAVEX Labs publishes three focused browser workspaces and deliberately public datasets for threat-intelligence analysts, defenders, investigators, journalists and researchers. It is maintained by Deividas Lis / HECAVEX on a best-effort basis without continuous monitoring, operational response or an SLA.

Publication gate

A workspace must answer a defined analytical question, expose public-source provenance, maintain a stable data boundary and explain what its output does not prove. Private submissions, credentials, malware samples, victim data, quarantined observations and unpublished analyst notes do not belong in the public build.

Evidence classes and attribution

Observation
A directly captured or source-reported fact with time and provenance.
Derivation
A reproducible transformation such as normalization, hashing, filtering or counting.
Assessment
An analyst interpretation that states confidence, competing explanations and limits.
Reference
Third-party framework or catalogue material attributable to its authoritative publisher.

Primary and authoritative sources are preferred. Infrastructure overlap, ATT&CK reference data, a shared tool or a similar page does not independently establish common control or actor identity. Generated counts and relationships identify their input, version and transformation.

Freshness and maintenance

Catalogue entries expose a state, owner, last review, review trigger and archive condition. Curated material is reviewed when its source changes, a correction arrives or related HECAVEX research is updated. “Maintained” means active best-effort stewardship, not real-time completeness.

Archive policy

Generated from upstream identifies a reviewed named third-party version. Frozen preserves a reproducible version without routine additions. Archived retains a record with its stale state and maintenance boundary visible. Retired interfaces remain at their established URL when useful for citations, but leave primary navigation and point to the maintained replacement.

Licensing and privacy

HECAVEX-authored software is MIT licensed. Original curated text and data are CC BY 4.0 only where the data terms identify them as covered. MITRE ATT&CK, linked sources, trademarks and other third-party material retain their own terms. See the data catalogue, licensing boundary and repository data notice before reuse.

Public workspaces require no account. Filtering and interface state remain in the browser unless a page explicitly says otherwise. Production uses privacy-preserving aggregate analytics as described in the HECAVEX privacy policy.

Limitations

Public reporting is incomplete and uneven. The Atlas is selected rather than exhaustive, pivot cases are time-bounded and ATT&CK mappings require procedure-level evidence. Technical similarity is a lead rather than attribution. Labs is not a blocklist, attribution authority, SOC, scanner or incident-response platform.

Corrections and security

Meaningful additions, corrections, schema changes and archived workspaces are recorded on the Changes page. Corrections should identify the page, record and supporting evidence and can be sent through the HECAVEX contact channel. Report website vulnerabilities or accidental publication through the Labs security policy.
