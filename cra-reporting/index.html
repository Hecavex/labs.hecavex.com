Decision record

What is known now?

Use Unknown when the evidence is incomplete. An unknown trigger becomes an investigation task, not an assumed No.

1. Scope and role

First establish why this organisation and product could fall within the reporting duty.

Relevant economic-operator role?Manufacturer of the product with digital elements, or an open-source software steward where the CRA duty applies.
Product and territorial scope?The affected product with digital elements is within CRA scope and made available on the Union market.

2. Trigger evidence

Assess both tracks independently. One occurrence can require more than one workstream.

Reliable evidence of malicious exploitation?The vulnerability was exploited in a system without permission of the system owner. A public PoC or feed listing alone is not necessarily evidence of exploitation.
Severe effect on protected data or functions?The incident negatively affects, or is capable of negatively affecting, availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of sensitive or important data or functions.
Malicious-code criterion met?The incident led, or is capable of leading, to malicious code being introduced or executed in the product or a user system.

3. Awareness and ownership

The legal clock is tied to awareness. Preserve who knew what, when, and on what evidence.

If the duty applies

Separate reporting tracks

Both start with an early warning, but their final-report clocks differ.

Article 14(1) and (2)

Actively exploited vulnerability

  1. Early warning without undue delay and within 24 hours of awareness.
  2. Vulnerability notification within 72 hours unless the information was already supplied.
  3. Final report no later than 14 days after a corrective or mitigating measure becomes available, unless already supplied.

Article 14(3) and (4)

Severe incident

  1. Early warning without undue delay and within 24 hours of awareness.
  2. Incident notification within 72 hours unless the information was already supplied.
  3. Final report within one month after the incident notification, unless already supplied.
