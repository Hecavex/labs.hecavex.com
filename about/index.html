About the project

Small public workspaces for inspectable CTI.

HECAVEX Labs turns selected research and structured evidence into interfaces that make provenance, transformations and analytical limits visible.

PUBLIC · READ ONLY · MAINTAINED BEST EFFORT

What Labs is

Labs is the operational publication layer of HECAVEX. It currently maintains the Baltic Threat Atlas, the Evidence Pivot Workspace and an ATT&CK Evidence Explorer. Each answers a bounded question and links its output back to public evidence. Labs does not attempt to become a general threat platform, an exhaustive directory or a substitute for primary reporting.

Audience and use

The workspaces are intended for CTI analysts, defenders, investigators, journalists and researchers who need to inspect how a claim was formed. They can support research and defensive discussion, but do not provide monitoring, incident response, takedown or an operational SLA.

Selection gate

A proposed workspace must add a distinct HECAVEX-specific analytical function, have a maintainable public dataset, preserve provenance and explain what its output cannot prove. A generic catalogue or an interface already served better by a maintained community tool does not qualify merely because it can be built.

Place in the HECAVEX network

HECAVEX Research ↗ publishes long-form investigations. Radar ↗ publishes bounded potential-phishing signals. APT Notes ↗ preserves structured actor, campaign and technique records. Labs provides inspectable interfaces for selected datasets and research methods across that portfolio.

Ownership and maintenance

Labs is created and maintained by Deividas Lis / HECAVEX. Maintenance is best effort. Dataset states, meaningful revisions and retirements are visible in the HECAVEX data catalogue and Changes; silent claims of continuous freshness are avoided.

Public, privacy and security boundaries

Labs requires no account. Public pages do not accept file uploads or analyst submissions. The publication build excludes private notes, credentials, victim data, malware samples and quarantined observations. Read the methodology, licensing boundary, privacy policy ↗ and security policy.

Corrections and contact

Send sourced editorial corrections and collaboration enquiries through the HECAVEX contact page ↗. Security reports and accidental-publication concerns follow the separate Labs security process.
