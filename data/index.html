Publication and lifecycle boundary

This catalogue excludes private notes, credentials, malware samples, victim data, submissions and quarantined observations. Deployment is default-deny: only validated public paths are staged, and each distribution must map to a catalogue entry and licence boundary. Maintained, generated, frozen and archived are publication states, not claims of continuous availability or complete intelligence coverage.

Machine access. Use catalogue.json for the complete record and public-manifest.json for the staged-file boundary. Report an exposure through the Labs security policy.
